# Group calendar

The **Calendar** tab helps group members follow upcoming Events, voting deadlines, and outcome review dates. Open an entry to return to its work in Loomio. Use the month, week, or day view, or choose **Upcoming** for a list.

<!-- translation-section: subscribe -->

## Subscribe in your calendar app

Choose **Subscribe to calendar**, then copy your calendar link. Add it as a subscription in your calendar app:

- **Google Calendar:** On the website, choose **Other calendars → From URL** and paste the link.
- **Outlook:** Choose **Add calendar → Subscribe from web** and paste the link.
- **Apple Calendar:** Choose **File → New Calendar Subscription** and paste the link.

Subscribe using the link to receive future changes. Importing a downloaded file creates a snapshot that does not receive updates.

The subscription is read-only. Its entries link back to Loomio, where you can participate in discussions and decisions. To confirm attendance at an Event, respond to its invitation or [vote in the Event poll](/en/user_manual/polls/events#confirm-attendance).

<!-- translation-section: privacy-and-updates -->

## Privacy and updates

Keep your calendar link private: anyone with the link can read the calendar details it exports, including your calendar provider. The feed contains work you can currently access in that group and omits participant email addresses, votes, and discussion bodies.

You can reset your link at any time. The old link stops working, and you will need to subscribe again with the new one. Leaving the group or losing access stops future calendar retrieval; copies already downloaded can remain in your calendar app.

Your calendar app controls refresh timing, so changes can take time to appear. Continue to use invitations and Loomio notifications for time-sensitive changes.
