# Frequently used emoji

The emoji picker now adapts to the emoji you choose most often. Its frequently
used row starts with eight common emoji, then reorders and expands as you use
the picker. Up to 16 of your most frequently chosen emoji are kept one click
away.

Usage is saved to your account and applies anywhere the emoji picker appears,
including reactions and the text editor. Each displayed emoji is counted
separately, so skin tone variants can appear as distinct frequently used
choices.
