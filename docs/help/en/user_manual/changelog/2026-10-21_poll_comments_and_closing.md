Thread members can now read poll comments in the thread, search, and exports regardless of whether poll results are visible. In polls that hide results until a vote is cast, members can also reply to votes before voting.

In polls that hide results until voting closes, members can comment on the poll and reply to comments while voting is open. Replies to votes become available after the poll closes. Vote choices and reasons remain hidden until closing.

Thread admins can extend the closing time while these polls are open, but cannot reopen them after closing has revealed the results. This applies to identified and anonymous polls. Other identified polls can still be reopened.
