# Confirm email address changes

Changing your account email address now requires confirmation from the new address. Select **Change email address** on your profile, enter the new address, and follow the link sent there. You can confirm from any browser without signing in. Your existing email remains active until you confirm. The link expires after 24 hours, and a new request replaces an earlier one. Loomio also notifies your old address when a change is requested and completed. Sites that manage profile details through single sign-on keep email editing disabled.
