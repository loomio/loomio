# Simpler notifications and an installable web app

Loomio now uses the same notification choices for account defaults, groups, and threads: **Catch-up only**, **When notified**, and **All activity**. New accounts start with **When notified** and a daily catch-up. Existing notification preferences have been retained.

## Mentions follow group and thread settings

The separate **Notify me when I'm mentioned** account setting has been removed. Mentions are notifications and now follow your email and push settings for the relevant thread or group.

With **When notified**, Loomio sends an update when someone mentions you, replies to you, shares a discussion with you, invites or reminds you to vote, announces an outcome, or when a poll is closing soon. With **Catch-up only**, those notifications are collected into your next catch-up instead. Choose **All activity** for a group or thread when you want every update as it happens.

These settings belong to each member. Group coordinators cannot change another member's notification preferences.

## Catch-up includes notifications

The catch-up now begins with unseen notifications, followed by unread discussions and polls. This makes it possible to receive your Loomio notifications and everything else you missed together in one daily, every-second-day, or weekly email.

When action is needed, the subject highlights votes you still need to cast, people who mentioned or replied to you, and polls closing soon. Closed polls, completed votes, and activity you can no longer access are not presented as pending actions.

Opening or previewing the catch-up does not mark its contents as read. Select **Mark catch-up as read** to acknowledge the notifications and topic activity included in the email.

## Installable web app and browser push

Loomio can now be installed from supported browsers and opened from a home screen or app launcher in its own window. Select **Install** from **Notification settings** when the option is available. On an iPhone or iPad, open Loomio in Safari and use **Share** then **Add to Home Screen**. The installed web app still requires an internet connection.

Loomio can also send push notifications through supported browsers. Enable push separately in each browser or installed app, then use **Test connection** to verify delivery to your enabled browsers.

After push is enabled, group and thread settings let you choose **Email**, **Push**, or **Email and push**. Email and push can use different notification levels. Notifications remain available under the bell in Loomio regardless of the external delivery method you choose.

The installable web app and browser push provide a foundation for future mobile notifications. This release does not include a native mobile app.
