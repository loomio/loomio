# Email links open notification settings for the message

The **Unsubscribe** link in a notification email now opens the settings for its thread or group, including emails about mentions and replies. A catch-up email opens a separate catch-up schedule form. You can use these email-linked forms without signing in, and the thread or group form shows your current catch-up schedule with a link to change it. The full **Notification settings** page in Loomio still requires sign-in.
