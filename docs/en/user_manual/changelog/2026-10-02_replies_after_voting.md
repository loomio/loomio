## Discuss votes as soon as you have voted

In polls that hide results until a vote is cast, people can now reply to votes as soon as they have voted. All comments under the poll stay hidden in the thread from people who have not voted, including direct comments and replies to replies.

Polls that hide results until voting closes keep comments under the poll hidden and replies and mentions in vote reasons disabled until closing. Anonymous polls continue to have no replies to individual votes.

Print, Markdown, and other exports no longer require you to vote first. They include results and replies from polls that hide results until voting, while results hidden until closing remain protected.
