# A complete Oatmilk Cooperative demo group

**Start a demo group** now creates an Oatmilk Cooperative board workspace for the signed-in user. It includes connected examples of sense checking and final consent, meeting preparation and scheduling, approval of previous minutes, strategy prioritisation, and a two-seat STV board election. New installations enable demo groups in the supplied environment template; existing installations can opt in with `FEATURES_DEMO_GROUPS=1`.

The demo includes completed decisions as well as current votes to cast, unread notifications, fictional colleagues, and example board documents. The user who starts it becomes a coordinator and can explore the normal group, thread, poll, and notification controls. Returning to **Start a demo group** opens the same demo while it remains available; demo groups continue to expire after seven days.
