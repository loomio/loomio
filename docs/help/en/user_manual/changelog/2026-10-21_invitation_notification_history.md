# Invitation delivery history

Group admins can now open **Invitation delivery history** from a person's menu on the Members page to see recorded invitations, resends, and delivery status chips. Expand an invitation to read its custom message and delivery details. The history remains available after the person joins. It records completed sends separately from linked bounce or complaint feedback when available.

Permanent bounces and spam complaints now have individual records linked to the email delivery when known. Temporary delivery failures do not permanently block an address. Signing in with a password, email code, or email link continues to clear Loomio's blocks for the person's current email address. Instance administrators can review and clear Loomio blocks from the user's admin page; the email service may enforce a separate block.
