# Notifications in catch-up email

The catch-up email now begins with unseen notifications, followed by the existing roundup of unread discussions and polls. It can be sent when there are unseen notifications even if there are no unread threads.

The subject highlights up to three kinds of pending activity in this order: votes you still need to cast, people who mentioned or replied to you, and polls closing soon. Poll access and voting eligibility are checked when the catch-up is prepared, so closed polls, completed votes, and activity you can no longer access are not presented as pending actions.

Opening or previewing the email no longer marks its contents as read. Select **Mark catch-up as read** to acknowledge the notifications and topic activity included in that email.

Group, thread, and default notification forms now ask **How do you want to stay updated?** The options are **Catch-up only**, **When notified**, and **All activity**. When the catch-up is disabled, **Catch-up only** becomes **No email updates** or **No push updates**. New accounts start with a daily catch-up and **When notified** email and push volumes. Existing notification preferences are unchanged.
