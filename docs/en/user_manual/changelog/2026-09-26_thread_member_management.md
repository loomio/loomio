# Manage thread members in one window

People who can invite others to a thread can now search its member list and invite or notify people from the same window. The member list shows 50 people per page and stays within a scrollable window. Selecting recipients shows the optional message field and hides the member list until the selection is cleared.

The form explains that new people are invited and existing thread members receive another notification. Guidance notices can be dismissed, and Loomio remembers each dismissal for the signed-in account.
