# Clearer suggestions when inviting people

When you invite people to a thread or a poll, your group now appears first in the suggestions. Delegates of the group come next, then connected chat rooms, then subgroups and the parent group.

**Everyone in the thread** is now offered only in direct threads. In a group thread, choose the group instead.

The **Manage voters** window now fits its content instead of opening at a fixed height. See [Manage voters](/en/user_manual/polls/manage_voters).
