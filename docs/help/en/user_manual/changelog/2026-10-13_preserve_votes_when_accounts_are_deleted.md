Votes with reasons now remain part of a poll and continue to count when a participant deletes or deactivates their account, including while a poll is still open. The poll's settings still control when its results become visible. Deleting an account removes the participant's name and personal information from their contributions.

Deleting or deactivating an account removes group access and prevents further voting. Leaving a group or being removed by an admin continues to remove the participant's votes from open polls.
