# Weighted quorum and vote share requirements

Quorum previously counted people even when weighted voting was enabled. Vote share requirements based on eligible voters also counted people, while requirements based on votes cast used weighted totals.

Weighted voting now applies consistently to quorum, participation percentages, and vote share requirements. Quorum and requirements based on **Eligible vote weight** include the weight assigned to eligible voters who have not yet voted. Requirements based on **Vote weight cast** use submitted weighted totals. Results, emails, and chat messages label these percentages to show their basis, alongside the plain vote counts. Selecting an unweighted results column switches the chart's participation percentage to people; selecting a weighted column switches it back to vote weight. Quorum and vote share requirements continue to use weights.

This applies automatically to existing weighted polls and polls started from weighted templates, including results of closed polls. No additional setting or permission is required. Polls without weighted voting continue to count people. See [Weighted voting](/en/user_manual/polls/weighted_voting).
