# Archived groups stop activity and notifications

Archived groups and their subgroups now block content access and changes, voting, announcements, reminders, and invitation acceptance. This also applies to saved poll links and requests from pages opened before archival.

Pending activity notifications are suppressed, including emails, push notifications, and chatbot messages. Messages already being sent may still arrive. Polls still close at their scheduled time.

Existing permissions for restoration, group export, permanent deletion, and access removal remain available. Direct discussions and polls keep their existing permissions.
