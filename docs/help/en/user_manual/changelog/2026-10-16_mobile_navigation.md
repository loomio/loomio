# Mobile navigation and faster page opening

The mobile bottom bar now has padded icons for the sidebar, dashboard, and notifications, with a thread menu button while reading a thread. Search is available from the dashboard. The mobile title bar shows the page title when its heading scrolls out of view, with a loading indicator at the top right.

Pages show content already held in the current session while refreshing it. Loomio also loads the first dashboard page, recent polls awaiting your vote, and notifications in the background after startup. Background loading leaves notification read state unchanged and does not grant access to additional content.

Threads now open at the start, with a **Latest** button to jump to recent activity. Links to a specific comment or poll still open that item.
