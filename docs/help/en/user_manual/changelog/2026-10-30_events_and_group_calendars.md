# Events and group calendars

Use an **Event** poll to schedule an event and confirm who’s coming. People can respond with **Going**, **Maybe**, or **Not going** in Loomio or through a compatible calendar invitation. A Time poll outcome can start an Event with the chosen time and location filled in.

Event invitations collect attendance responses and support schedule updates and cancellations. **No response** means someone has not confirmed attendance; email delivery information remains separate. Changes to the event time request fresh confirmations.

Group members can use the new **Calendar** tab to see Events, voting deadlines, and outcome review dates. Choose **Subscribe to calendar** to add a private calendar feed to Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, or another supporting app. Each member has a separate link that they can reset.
