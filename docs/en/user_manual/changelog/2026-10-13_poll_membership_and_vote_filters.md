# Poll lists follow your membership and pending votes

The **Need your vote** filter now follows your own voting status. A poll stays on the list when someone else votes and leaves it when you vote. Anonymous polls use whether you have submitted your ballot.

When you leave a group or an admin removes you, private polls in that group and its subgroups disappear from your open dashboard and voting list, including in other tabs. Public polls remain readable, but leaving removes your voting rights. This also applies when you leave while a poll is closed and it is later reopened.

Rejoining restores access and existing votes in active polls without requiring a page refresh.
