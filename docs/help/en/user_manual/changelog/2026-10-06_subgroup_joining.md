# Subgroup visibility and joining without approval

Subgroup admins can let parent group members find and join a subgroup without approval while keeping it hidden from outsiders. In **Edit group settings → Privacy**, select **Visible to parent group**, then **Members of [parent group] can join without approval** under **How do people join?**. These settings are also available when creating a subgroup.

Members can leave and rejoin while they still belong to the parent group. People outside the parent group need an invitation. Joining gives ordinary subgroup membership; it does not give admin rights or make private content public. Secret subgroups remain invitation only.

Open and Closed subgroups can also allow immediate joining. Because they are publicly visible, anyone can join when that option is selected.

The new visibility label makes the audience explicit. Existing Closed subgroups hidden under a private parent now show **Visible to parent group**, with their existing access unchanged. These subgroups stay hidden from outsiders if the parent becomes public. Making a parent private restricts its public subgroups and their threads, while preserving Secret subgroups.

A Secret parent group supports only **Visible to parent group** and **Secret** subgroups. Open and Closed subgroups require a public parent group.

Guests invited to a parent group's threads do not gain access to subgroups visible only to parent members.
