## Group deletion and inactive subscriptions

Deleting a group now uses the same recoverable discard state as other Loomio content. Group administrators and instance administrators warn the group's administrators and schedule permanent deletion after two weeks. Instance administrators can still delete spam or explicitly delete a group immediately. The discard time and the person who requested deletion are recorded for audit.

Groups with expired, cancelled, past-due or on-hold subscriptions are unavailable for viewing and participation. Groups without a subscription remain available as free groups, and direct discussions and polls are unaffected.
