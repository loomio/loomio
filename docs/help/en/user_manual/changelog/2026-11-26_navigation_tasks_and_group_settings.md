# Redesigned navigation, group settings, and tasks

**All groups** (your dashboard) and group pages now share a unified navigation bar with cards for **Threads**, **Polls**, **Members** (or **People** on **All groups**), **Files**, **Tasks**, and **Bookmarks**, plus a dedicated **Settings** section on group pages.

- **Dedicated Group Settings section**: Group options have moved out of the header menu into a **Settings** card on the group page, grouped into **Group administration**, **Your membership**, **Integrations & data**, and **Leave or delete**. Each option appears only when you have permission to use it.
- **Simplified sidebar and title switcher**: The sidebar now focuses on **All groups**, your groups and subgroups, and starting new groups or subgroups. When the sidebar is closed, select the page title (**All groups** or the group name) to switch groups or start a new group.
- **All groups People directory**: Open **People** on **All groups** to search people across your groups, see who was recently online, view profiles, or start a direct thread.
- **Opt-in subgroup filtering**: On parent group pages, **Threads**, **Polls**, **Members**, **Files**, **Tasks**, and **Bookmarks** now default to **This group only**. Use the **Subgroups** menu to switch to **My subgroups** or **All subgroups**, or jump directly to a subgroup.
- **Redesigned Tasks**: Open **Tasks** on **All groups** or on any group page to filter tasks by status (**To do**, **Done**, **All**), due date (**Overdue**, **Next 7 days**, **Later**, **No due date**), assignee (on group pages), subgroup, category tag, or search.
- **Unread and Direct filters**: Filter **All groups → Threads** by **Unread** or **Direct**, or use the **Unread** filter on any group's **Threads** page. Unread counts and the **Unread** filter cover threads with activity in the last six weeks.
- **Shareable invite links and trial extension**: Shareable group invite links now include an **Include intro message** option to copy a ready-to-send invitation message. Free trial groups unlock 30 extra days—with subgroups and guests enabled—once three people vote in a proposal or poll.
