Users can now follow updates from open Groups without becoming members. Select **Follow updates** on an open Group page to include its unread discussions, comments, polls, and other thread activity in your regular catch-up email.

Following a Group does not grant membership or permission to participate. The Group's **Join group** option remains available when its membership settings allow joining or requesting membership. Groups that can only be joined by invitation can still be followed when their discussions are public.

Followed threads use the same read status and catch-up schedule as other threads. Select **Unfollow updates** on the Group page to stop receiving them in the catch-up.
