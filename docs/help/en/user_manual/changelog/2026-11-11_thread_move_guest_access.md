# Keep people participating when moving a thread

When you move a thread to another group or subgroup, Loomio shows how many people would lose permission to participate. If you can invite guests in the destination group, you can keep those people as guests of the thread. They can continue participating without joining the destination group or receiving invitation notifications.

Inviting someone who has already read a thread now correctly grants them guest access when needed. This also restores commenting access for people who were notified before a thread moved out of their group.
