# Invitation limit for accounts without a paid group

On Loomio sites with subscription billing, people who do not belong to an active paid group have an invitation allowance of 10 distinct people across groups, threads and polls. If you belong to an active trial group with a larger member allowance, your invitation allowance matches it. If you belong to several trial groups, the highest allowance applies. A trial with unlimited members also allows unlimited invitees.

You can continue sending invitations, announcements and reminders to the same people without using more slots. The limit is based on invitation records that still exist; it does not reset each day. Existing group membership limits still apply.

Automatic updates to existing discussion participants are not counted. People who belong to an active paid group and private installations without subscription billing keep their existing invitation limits.
