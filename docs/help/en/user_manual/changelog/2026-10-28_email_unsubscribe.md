# Change catch-up settings from an unsubscribe link

The **Unsubscribe** link in a notification email now lets you change your catch-up schedule on the same page as the thread or group settings, without signing in. The catch-up section is labelled **Catch-up emails (all groups)** because its schedule applies across your groups.

Choose **Catch-up only** in the thread or group settings to stop immediate emails while keeping your catch-up. Save those settings separately from any change to the catch-up schedule.
